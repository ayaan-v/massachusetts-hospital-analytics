# Massachusetts General Hospital Analytics
### Examining patient encounters, treatment costs, and behavioral patterns | 2011 – 2022


## Project Overview

This is an end-to-end data analytics project built on a synthetic Electronic Health Record (EHR) dataset generated by **Synthea** for Massachusetts General Hospital. The project covers the full analytics workflow — data profiling in Excel, exploratory analysis in Python, structured querying in MySQL, and interactive dashboard development in Power BI.

The analysis is structured around three core objectives:
- **Encounters Overview** — understanding volume, class distribution, and duration patterns
- **Cost & Coverage** — identifying cost drivers, payer performance, and coverage gaps
- **Patient Behavior** — profiling patient demographics and readmission patterns

> **Note:** All cost figures in this dataset are synthetically generated by Synthea and do not reflect real-world hospital billing rates. The analytical patterns and relationships between variables remain valid for portfolio and learning purposes.

## Phase 1 — Excel

Built an 8-sheet workbook covering:

- **Data Profiling** — null checks, row counts, and dataset summary across all 5 tables
- **Analysis** — 5 formula-driven summary tables (encounters by year, class breakdown, avg claim cost by payer, top 10 procedures, duration split)

## Phase 2 — Python EDA

Five analytical sections covering insights that go beyond Excel's capabilities:

**Section 1 — 30-Day Readmission Analysis**
- 79.1% of patients were readmitted within 30 days of a previous encounter
- Readmission count distribution and top 10 most readmitted patients

**Section 2 — Patient Demographics Deep Dive**
- Age group distribution with cross-tabulation by gender
- Age statistics — mean 73 years, heavily skewed toward senior patients (65+)

**Section 3 — Quarterly Patient Trend**
- Unique patients per quarter across the full 11-year period
- Peak and trough quarters identified

**Section 4 — Cost Distribution & Correlation**
- Percentile breakdown of claim costs (median: significantly lower than mean — right-skewed distribution)
- Payer coverage rate analysis — Medicaid provides highest coverage rate
- Correlation matrix across cost and duration variables

**Section 5 — Procedure Cost Analysis**
- Top 10 procedures by average base cost
- Procedure cost percentile breakdown

## Phase 3 — SQL (MySQL)

**Database:** `hosp_analysis`  
**Total Queries:** 9 across 3 objectives

### Objective 1 — Encounters Overview
| # | Question | Techniques Used |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | Total encounters per year | GROUP BY, COUNT |
| 1b | Encounter class % per year | Window function — SUM OVER PARTITION BY |
| 1c | Over vs under 24 hours split | TIMESTAMPDIFF, CASE, Subquery |

### Objective 2 — Cost & Coverage
| # | Question | Techniques Used |
|---|---|---|
| 2a | Zero payer coverage count & % | WHERE filter, Subquery |
| 2b | Top 10 procedures by frequency | GROUP BY, ORDER BY, LIMIT |
| 2c | Top 10 procedures by avg base cost | GROUP BY, AVG, ORDER BY, LIMIT |
| 2d | Avg claim cost by payer | JOIN, GROUP BY, AVG |

### Objective 3 — Patient Behavior
| # | Question | Techniques Used |
|---|---|---|
| 3a | Unique patients per quarter | YEAR, QUARTER, COUNT DISTINCT |
| 3b | 30-day readmission count | CTE, Self JOIN, DATEDIFF |
| 3c | Most readmitted patients | CTE, Self JOIN, JOIN patients, COUNT |

## Phase 4 — Power BI Dashboard

3-page interactive dashboard with consistent light theme (white/navy), KPI cards, slicers, and page navigation.

### Page 1 — Encounters Overview
- **KPIs:** Total Encounters, Unique Patients, Total Procedures, % Over 24 Hours
- **Visuals:** Encounter Class by Year (stacked bar), Total Encounters by Year (line), Encounter Duration Split (donut)
- **Slicers:** Year, Encounter Class

### Page 2 — Cost & Coverage
- **KPIs:** Avg Total Claim Cost, Zero Coverage Encounters, % Zero Coverage, Total Payer Coverage
- **Visuals:** Avg Claim Cost by Payer (bar), Total Claim Cost by Payer (donut), Top Procedures by Frequency (bar), Avg Claim Cost by Age Group (treemap)
- **Slicers:** Year, Payer

### Page 3 — Patient Behavior
- **KPIs:** Avg Encounters per Patient, Most Common Age Group, Readmitted Patients (30 Days), Female Patients %
- **Visuals:** Unique Patients per Quarter (line), Unique Patients by Year and Gender (clustered bar), Patient Age Group Distribution (bar), Patient Gender Distribution (bar)
- **Slicers:** Year, Gender
